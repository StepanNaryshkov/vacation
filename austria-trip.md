- **Base:** Strobl am Wolfgangsee (single base, all day-trips)
- **Length:** ~4 nights — **arrive day** (late afternoon) + **3 full days** + **departure day** (morning)

## Booking & prep (Austria-specific)
- [ ] Confirm **Hohenwerfen falconry** times (target **11:15** on the castle day).
- [ ] **Verify Hallstatt funicular/Skywalk** status (closed for rebuild, reopening "summer 2026"; ground-level Classic View unaffected).
- [ ] Check **Wolfgangsee boat** timetable from Strobl (end of the line = fewer sailings).
- [ ] Reserve a **Salzburg dinner** spot for the city evening if wanted.
- [ ] Confirm rental includes the **Austrian digital vignette** (needed for the A10 on the Hohenwerfen/Salzburg day); if not, buy online.
- [ ] **Zwölferhorn cable car** (optional, St. Gilgen) — check prices (~€30–36 adult return, family tickets) + last descent; only worth it in clear weather.
- [ ] Local rules to plan around: **shops closed Sundays**; Salzburg **on-street parking free Sundays/holidays and after ~19:00**.

## Local food & desserts (casual)
- **Strobl (base):** traditional **Gasthof/Wirtshaus** for **Reinanke/Saibling** (Wolfgangsee whitefish), schnitzel + Kaiserschmarrn; a village **Bäckerei** for breakfast; a lakeside café for evening gelato ([Gasthöfe in Strobl](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gasthof+Strobl+Wolfgangsee) — confirm on the spot). More choice 10 min away at [Im Weissen Rössl](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Im+Weissen+R%C3%B6ssl+St.+Wolfgang), St. Wolfgang.
- **Salzburg:** [Café Tomaselli](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caf%C3%A9+Tomaselli+Salzburg) (classic coffee house + cakes) or [Café Fürst](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caf%C3%A9+F%C3%BCrst+Salzburg) (original Mozartkugel); grab a **Bosna** sausage at [Balkan Grill Walter](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Balkan+Grill+Walter+Salzburg) on Getreidegasse; try **Salzburger Nockerl**; beer-garden dinner at [Augustiner Bräustübl](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Augustiner+Br%C3%A4ust%C3%BCbl+M%C3%BClln).
- **Bad Ischl:** [Konditorei Zauner](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Konditorei+Zauner+Bad+Ischl) — **Zaunerstollen** + imperial cakes.
- **Hallstatt:** lakeside [Bräugasthof](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Br%C3%A4ugasthof+Hallstatt) or [Seewirt Zauner](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Seewirt+Zauner+Hallstatt) for fresh **Reinanke** (lake fish).
- **Mondsee:** [Café Frauenschuh](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caf%C3%A9+Frauenschuh+Mondsee) for cakes; local **Mondseer cheese**.
- **St. Wolfgang / St. Gilgen:** [Im Weissen Rössl](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Im+Weissen+R%C3%B6ssl+St.+Wolfgang); gelato on the lake promenade.

## Decisions log (in / out and why)
- **OUT — Altaussee:** ~2 h loop + cold water; redundant with warmer lakes.
- **OUT — Gollinger Wasserfall:** wet, slippery steps; awkward with a baby.
- **OUT — salt mines / ice caves:** minimum-age limits; Hallstatt's closed anyway.
- **OUT — Zell am See:** different region, ~1.5 h each way.
- **OUT — SchafbergBahn:** too expensive (~€100+/family); Gosausee already delivers the mountain-lake view.

# Austria Trip — WEATHER-ADAPTED (wet early days)

Alternative to [austria-trip.md](./austria-trip.md), reshuffled around a forecast where the **arrive day + Day 1 are stormy** and **Days 2–3 are dry**. Same base/car/kids rules ([AGENTIC.md](./AGENTIC.md)).

## Forecast pattern this variant assumes
Re-check ~2 days before travelling; use this layout whenever the first days are wet and the later ones dry.

| Day | Conditions | High | Rain | Verdict |
|-----|-----------|------|------|---------|
| Arrive day | **Severe storms** (20–45 mm) | 29–31 °C | 70–90% | very wet — travel/settle |
| **Day 1** | Thunderstorms (5–17 mm) | 23–24 °C | **60–85%** | ☔ wet |
| Day 2 | Dry, sunny | 27–29 °C | ~5–15% | ☀ best day |
| Day 3 | Dry, hot | 30–33 °C | ~18–29% | ☀ great (slight PM shower risk) |
| Departure day | Showers | 25–26 °C | ~49–67% | showery |

## What changed vs the original
- **Day 1 (likely wet) → indoor/town: Bad Ischl (Kaiservilla, indoor) + Hallstatt village** (atmospheric + uncrowded in rain). **Gosausee dropped from Day 1** (needs a clear sky for the reflection).
- **Gosausee → moved to the clearest of Day 2/3.**
- **Salzburg stays an evening** (Day 3) — you keep the lit-up old town + dinner, and it's less driving than a Salzburg day-trip.
- **Northern-lakes sightseeing trimmed** → evening swims at Strobl.
- *Alternative:* if you'd rather a fully-indoor Day 1, do **Salzburg (city)** instead and move Hallstatt+Gosausee to Day 2.

## Trade-off & flex
- **Hallstatt in rain is fine** — the Classic View is at lake level, it's atmospheric and far less crowded; café-hop if a thunderstorm rolls through. **Bad Ischl (Kaiservilla) is fully indoor**, so Day 1 works wet *or* dry.
- **Gosausee is the one weather-gamble** — do it on the clearest day (needs a clear sky + it's exposed); check the morning webcam.
- **Keeps the Salzburg evening** + less driving than a Salzburg day-trip.
- **If Day 1 turns dry**, just add Gosausee back to Day 1 = the original [austria-trip.md](./austria-trip.md).
- *Fully-indoor Day 1 alternative:* swap to **Salzburg (city)**, Hallstatt+Gosausee to Day 2.

# Slovenia Trip — Zgornje Gorje base (Bled area)

Concrete plan. Traveler profile + reusable rules live in [AGENTIC.md](./AGENTIC.md).

## Overview
- **Base:** **Zgornje Gorje** (village ~4 km NW of Bled, right next to **Vintgar Gorge**) — whole stay
- **Length:** ~6 days — **arrive day** + **5 full days** + **departure day** (~2 h 15 drive in from Austria)
- **Wants this trip:** the **sea (a kid-friendly, shallow *paddling* beach)**, **one Austrian lake**, an **evening in Ljubljana**, plus Bled-area highlights.

## Options & swaps
- **Sea (kid-friendly Slovenian coast, ~2.2 h):** no sand in Slovenia, but **Simonov zaliv (Izola)** is the best family beach — **shallow, gentle paddling, grassy areas, Blue Flag, lifeguards**; ideal for the baby + 8-year-old. **Portorož** is the alternative (managed, shallow, some imported sand, most facilities). *(Real sand would mean Italy/Grado at ~3 h — dropped to keep it local + shorter.)*
- **Early-beach plan (coast hits ~35 °C in August):** pre-pack the night before; leave **~06:00**; on the beach by ~08:15 while it's cool and empty; head back before the midday heat; nap + a Bled/Bohinj swim in the afternoon.
- **Austrian lake:** **Wörthersee** (Pyramidenkogel + Pörtschach/Velden beaches — matches your pins) or **Faaker See** (most turquoise, a bit closer).
- **Big day (Day 5):** swap the south/caves for a **Soča Valley day** if you prefer mountains — [Kranjska Gora](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kranjska+Gora) + [Lake Jasna](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Jezero+Jasna) + [Vršič Pass](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Vr%C5%A1i%C4%8D+Pass) + [Great Soča Gorge](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Velika+korita+So%C4%8De). *(Then do Ljubljana as its own short evening — it's only ~50 min from Bled.)*

## Local food & desserts (casual)
- **Bled:** the famous **kremšnita** (cream cake) at [Slaščičarna Šmon](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sla%C5%A1%C4%8Di%C4%8Darna+%C5%A0mon+Bled) or lakeside [Park Café](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Park+Caf%C3%A9+Bled) (the original recipe).
- **Ljubljana:** cakes + gelato at [Kavarna Zvezda](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kavarna+Zvezda+Ljubljana) or riverside [Cacao](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cacao+Ljubljana); casual **Kranjska klobasa** at [Klobasarna](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Klobasarna+Ljubljana); traditional [Gostilna Sokol](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gostilna+Sokol+Ljubljana).
- **Bohinj:** lakeside gostilna for local **trout**; ice-cream by the pier.
- **Izola / Portorož (beach day):** fresh **seafood** + gelato on the promenade ([Manzioli, Izola](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Manzioli+Izola)).

## Booking & prep (Slovenia-specific)
- [ ] **Slovenian e-vinjeta** (motorway vignette) — required, separate from Austria's. Buy online before driving.
- [ ] **Beach day stays in Slovenia** (Izola/Portorož) — just the e-vinjeta; coast is **hot (~35 °C)**, so go early + bring shade.
- [ ] **Vintgar Gorge** — timed/limited entry in summer; arrive early or pre-book.
- [ ] **Postojna Cave** (if doing it) — pre-book; it's **~10 °C inside** → warm layers for the baby; pricey (budget note).
- [ ] **Ljubljana** old town is a pedestrian/ZTL zone — park in a **garage** and walk; evening = cooler + easier parking.
- [ ] Confirm **Austrian vignette** still covers the Wörthersee day.

## Kid/baby notes
- Great swims: **Bled** and **Bohinj** (both swimmable, grassy lidos), Wörthersee, the sea.
- **Beach day:** **Simonov zaliv** has shallow, gentle entry (great for **paddling**) + grass to sit on; go ~08:00, bring **shade + water** — the coast gets very hot midday.
- **Vintgar** = flat boardwalk (baby in carrier; some steps — no stroller). **Vršič pass** = 50 hairpins, winding. **Postojna** = cold.
- Bled cream cake (**kremšnita**) is a must with the 8-year-old.
